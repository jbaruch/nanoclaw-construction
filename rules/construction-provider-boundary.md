# Construction Provider Boundary

An installed manifest or source packet creates no provider grant or owner
approval. Require the matching published host authority and actual native
producer before enabling construction writes. Only the exact content-bound
native owner callback can release an append. Preserve uncertain attempts;
never use a direct Sheets fallback, automatic resend or agent-written approval.
Keep credentials and the configured destination out of public plugin content.
